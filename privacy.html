Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

This app is a private tool used only by its owner to upload videos to the owner's own accounts.

What is stored

  • The login tokens the platform issues after you allow access (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram).
  • Basic account information: account id and display name.

Tokens are kept only on the owner's private server and are used only to upload the owner's videos and to show which account is connected.

What is not done

  • No data is sold or shared with third parties.
  • No data of other users is collected.
  • This website itself uses no cookies, analytics or tracking.

Deleting your data

Remove the app's access in your platform account settings (for TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions). The stored tokens then stop working and are deleted by the owner.
